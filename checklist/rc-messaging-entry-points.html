Checklist Simulator

Recruit Messaging can be started from more places than you'd think. Check off each entry point as you confirm it.

Scenario: You want to message the 2026 Recruit Board's QBs and WRs, then follow up individually with one recruit later. Find both messaging entry points.

✓
Step 1From Recruit Management, select recruits and open the More menu → Send Message.
✓
Step 2From a Recruiting Board, filter to QBs and WRs, then message everyone shown — the Board's filters carry into the send.
✓
Step 3From Groups or Saved Searches, select one or more and message every member — editable right in the composer.
✓
Step 4Open the individual recruit's profile and click Messaging to jump straight into their 1:1 conversation.

✓ Recruit Messaging is available from profiles, Boards, Groups, Saved Searches, and Recruit Management — use whichever is fastest for the moment.
