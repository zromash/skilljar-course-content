Step through building a manual group.

Scenario: You want to organize a set of recruits into a group so you can follow up with them together.

Step 1 of 5
Click the Recruiting module, then select the Recruits tab.
This opens the Recruit Management page.
Step 2 of 5
Select the checkboxes next to the recruits you want to group.
You choose exactly who is in the group, since this is a manual group.
Step 3 of 5
Click the Action menu in the upper right and choose Add to Group.
A window opens with your existing groups and a Create New Group option.
Step 4 of 5
Check an existing group, or select Create New Group, name it, and click Save.
Either way you control where these recruits land.
Step 5 of 5
Click Add to Group to finalize.
The recruits are now in the group, ready for follow-up and communication.

Groups keep the right people together so the right recruits get the right information.
